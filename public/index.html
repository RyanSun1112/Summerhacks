Pulse
Live venue energy

A live map of how events feel — built from the people actually there.

When someone captures a moment, Pulse freezes a photo together with the venue’s live measured energy — motion, sync, and crowd reading from the phones in the room, not a typed rating.

That isn’t a static review left behind later. It’s a timestamped reading of how the place actually felt right then, taken by people who were there.

Every capture stays on a public city map. As more people check in, each venue’s story keeps growing — permanent, browsable, and open to anyone.
