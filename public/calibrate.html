Venue calibration

Fix two known points and this solves for the venue's real position, size and rotation, so venue.json can map GPS onto the map. Pick points that are far apart and physically unambiguous — opposite corners or gates are ideal.

Either stand there and capture (outdoors, still, clear sky), or type the coordinates straight in — right-click the spot in Google Maps and its "copy coordinates" output pastes in as-is. Typing is more accurate than a phone fix and you can do it at a desk.
